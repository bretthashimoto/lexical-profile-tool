Fine-grained bands (optional)

Split the most frequent words into narrower bands than the rest of the list, so a coverage curve doesn't lump e.g. the top 1000 words together when finer distinctions there matter most.


Coarse-grained bands (optional)

Collapse the long tail of least-frequent words into a handful of wide bands instead of many normal-size ones.
